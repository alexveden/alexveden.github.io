What gets built

Three phases, each ending in something running

The first build is the smallest desk that can trade for real, with up to three months from agreement to first live trade. It grows from there, without being rebuilt.

Phase 01

Research

Data ingestion, storage and a backtesting environment. You can test a strategy yourself, on your own data, before any money moves. Available on its own, including an audit and overhaul for teams that already trade.

Phase 02

Execution

Order management and broker connectivity. An order reaches the market, comes back filled, and the position is where it should be.

Phase 03

Risk and reporting

Position, P&L, limits and a daily report. The desk trades inside its limits and leaves a record of what it did.

How I work

One architect, no handoffs

  • Ownership

    One person designs and builds the pipeline. There is no integration seam between teams, because there are no teams.

  • Cadence

    A working demo every two weeks, not a status report.

  • Your repository

    Source, tests and documentation go into your repository.

  • Your server

    A shared machine for the team on your infrastructure: JupyterLab, VPN access, one environment for everyone.

  • Handover

    Runbooks, deployment notes and a walkthrough, so the desk does not depend on me being reachable.

What I've built

Three systems built on my code. All mid-frequency, all on real money.

2018 – 2022

Multi-account algorithmic fund

Problem: A fund trading futures, options and US stocks across several accounts had no single system from research to execution.

Build: A backtesting engine for ML models, the datafeed, multi-account execution via Trading Technologies FIX and Interactive Brokers, a Jupyter research environment over VPN, and the Linux servers.

Outcome: The fund co-managed over $40M on this framework.

2023 – 2026

Futures prop trading group

Problem: A prop group needed signal research and execution on US futures.

Build: My execution framework adapted for them, a tool that generates swarms of alpha signals, FIX execution to Trading Technologies, and their Linux server operations.

Outcome: Live trading on US futures through TT FIX.

2021 – now

My own book

Problem: My own capital needed the same research and execution standard I build for clients.

Build: An execution framework (multi-leg positions, internal netting, smart orders) and a research engine (universal datafeed, alpha library, Monte Carlo portfolio risk).

Outcome: It runs my own and family money, and every client build starts from it.
