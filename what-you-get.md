Every engagement is scoped as a working system, not a list of features. The steps differ
in how much of the stack you get and how much of it you keep running yourself. Each step
builds on the one before, so nothing gets rebuilt. All four are built the same way: one
architect, source in your repository, a working demo every two weeks.

## What is not included

These are left out on purpose, in every step. They are either your side of the desk or
someone else's regulated business.

- Alpha and strategy research, plus portfolio construction. You own the thesis; I build
  the research process that tests it.
- Compliance reporting, fund accounting, NAV and reconciliation against a prime broker.
- Broker onboarding, prime brokerage agreements and exchange membership.
- Regulatory licensing, legal or investment advice.
- On-site presence.

## Pricing

Every engagement is quoted per scope at a fixed fee, billed against milestones. The
number depends on the market, the broker, the instruments and how much of the stack you
want. A Research Workflow is quoted the same way, on its own or as the first step of a
desk. If the scope changes, it is requoted before any more work is done.

## Running costs

Every desk tier is built for mid-frequency, which runs on one machine. What it costs to
keep the desk running stays small, so your budget goes to research rather than
infrastructure.

<ul class="facts">
    <li>
        <span class="fact-label">One server</span>
        <p>A single machine, rented or in your rack, plus a standby if you need disaster
        recovery. Nothing to orchestrate.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <span class="fact-label">Market data and broker</span>
        <p>Your subscriptions and fees, in your name. They depend on the market and are
        covered during scoping.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <span class="fact-label">No infra team</span>
        <p>The desk runs without a dedicated DevOps person. Your headcount goes to research.</p>
    </li>
</ul>
